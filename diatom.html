Exploring the intricate beauty of microscopic algae

About Diatoms

Diatoms are a major group of microalgae and are among the most common types of phytoplankton. They are unicellular organisms that live in houses made of glass (silica). These microscopic algae are encased within a unique cell wall made of silica called a frustule, which displays a wide variety of intricate and beautiful patterns.
